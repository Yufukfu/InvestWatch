# 0308. M3 gate: a recommendation of instruments alone lifts the 0.4 cap ("may indicate" advice)

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-11-21
- Deciders: agent-written; accepted by Damian via PR #35

## Context

M3 flags offers by entities outside the KNF registry that may be investment advice (`openspec/specs/m3-unauthorized-advice-signals/spec.md`, "M3 score and hard gate"). Art. 76 of the Act on Trading in Financial Instruments, as quoted in the Topic 3 annex, defines advice as a recommendation concerning one or more financial instruments given on the basis of the client's needs and situation; the annex summarises it as "rekomendacje nabywania, zbywania albo trzymania instrumentów finansowych". The challenge description asks M3 to find offers "mogących wskazywać" on such services, not to establish that advice was given.

Two signals carry the definition: S3.4 (a recommendation to buy, sell or hold instruments of art. 2-3) and S3.3 (personalisation: the client's situation). The first plan (`.docs/analytics-plan.md` §6) capped the score at 0.4 unless S3.3 or S3.4 was present. Damian's answer from the challenge criteria (relayed by the coordinating session, 2026-11-21) was: personalisation together with instruments is strongest, instruments alone is strong, personalisation alone is weak, education is low. Kacper's review of PR #35 (item 8) noted that S3.4 alone scores at least 0.7 while art. 76 also requires the client's situation, and asked for the choice to be recorded.

## Options considered

1. **Full art. 76: cap unless S3.3 and S3.4 are both present.** Closest to the legal definition. Evidence: the mock `cdbf5306…` (a paid "Grupa VIP" telling followers what to buy and sell) has S3.3 `pass` in `contracts/mocks/findings.json`, so this option would never alert on paid signal groups, the most common unlicensed offer in the challenge material. Rules can rarely see personalisation in a public post at all.
2. **S3.4 alone lifts the cap (chosen).** A recommendation of instruments by an unregistered entity is a strong indication; personalisation raises the score further (weights S3.4 3.0, S3.3 1.5, design decision 10 of `analytics-m1-m3`). Evidence: with S3.4 confirmed, `cdbf5306…` scores ≈0.88 and an offer with both signals ≈0.92; personalisation alone stays at most 0.4 (`tests/test_m3.py`, `test_instrument_recommendation_alone_is_strong`, `test_personalisation_alone_is_weak`).
3. **No gate.** Rejected: education and service vocabulary alone (`72acbb3b…`) would reach the alert threshold with enough soft signals.

## Decision

Option 2. The M3 score is capped at 0.4 unless S3.4 is `fail` or `uncertain`; S3.3 does not lift the cap on its own. M3 output is framed as "may indicate" advice: titles say "możliwe …" while the deciding signal is only a rule candidate, and when S3.3 is not detected the summary says that personalisation (art. 76) is unconfirmed. The analyst, not the score, decides whether advice was given.

## Consequences

- Paid signal groups and buy/sell calls by unregistered entities reach the M3 threshold once the LLM confirms S3.4. Rules-only, an S3.4 candidate (`uncertain`) already opens the gate, so an offer with other strong signals can pass the threshold (the mock `007bbcee…` scores ≈0.90) while a bare signal group stays below it (`cdbf5306…` ≈0.62); both are shown as "możliwe …" and "do weryfikacji".
- A finding never claims that art. 76 is met; its summary names the missing element.
- If the gold set (`.docs/analytics-plan.md` §8) shows too many S3.4-only alerts, the weight of S3.4 or the gate is revisited in a new ADR that supersedes this one.
