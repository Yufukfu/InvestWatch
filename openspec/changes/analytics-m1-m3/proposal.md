# Proposal

**Stage 2 (etap 2).** This change depends on `analytics-worker` landing on `main` first. It is applied only after that change is merged, and its branch is rebased on it.

## Why

Analytics now routes only `firm_own` documents (to M2). Every `third_party` and `unknown` document is dropped with `module_not_implemented`, so the two UKNF questions about content published by someone else get no answer: M1 (ads of registered investment firms published by third parties or private persons) and M3 (offers that may indicate investment advice, Art. 76 of the Act on Trading in Financial Instruments, by entities outside the KNF registry). The skeleton also sends unregistered "own" pages to M2, although M2 is only for firms registered in Poland. Integration #2 (Sat 22:00) needs every module to produce findings from at least one source. The module definitions follow the challenge criteria (`doc/Opis Wyzwania HackNation UKNF 19.11.pdf` pp. 1-4 and the annexes), as Damian asked.

## What Changes

- **Routing by publisher and registry** (`.docs/analytics-plan.md` §2 step 4, tightened to the challenge): a publisher that is a registry firm (by profile or page domain, or by its handle) goes to M2 on any platform, even when the scraper marked the source `third_party`. A `firm_own` page with no registry match goes to M3 (or nowhere without advisory signals), never to M2. A third party mentioning a registry firm goes to M1. An unregistered publisher with advisory signals goes to M3, also alongside M1 when the advisory offer is the publisher's own (a finfluencer's buy/sell calls), but not when the offer belongs to the registered firm. Documents with no advisory signal and no registry firm produce no finding (`no_signals`). For third-party content the registry match also looks at link hosts. This replaces design decision 5 of the merged `analytics-skeleton`.
- **M1 as an inventory** (plan §4): signals S1.1-S1.7 as rules: registry match, affiliate or referral link and promo code, partnership label (an attribute: present raises the certainty, absent does not exonerate), call to action, author other than the firm, and targeting readers in Poland (S1.7, which also filters out ads not aimed at Poland, while keeping Polish-language ads of foreign firms). S1.5 (promotional vs educational) needs a model, so without one it is `uncertain` with no evidence. The M1 score is "how certain this is a third-party ad of registered firm X". The finding carries the required firm data, advertiser data, ad content and URL; the worker's upsert keeps it current on re-scans.
- **M3 by Art. 76** (plan §6): signals S3.1-S3.6 as rules: entity not in the registry, advisory-service vocabulary, paid model, targeting Poland. Personalization (S3.3) and a recommendation to buy, sell or hold instruments from Art. 2-3 (S3.4) need a model: rules report candidates as `uncertain`. Plain crypto-assets are not instruments; crypto CFDs, futures and options are. A buy/sell call on plain crypto only is S3.8 "poza art. 2", low and capped. S3.7 (KNF public warnings list) is in the catalogue as not implemented, and `advertiser.on_warning_list` stays `null`. The M3 finding carries entity data, offer content and URL.
- **Scores**: M1 and M3 use `σ(bias + Σ wᵢ·xᵢ)`, with hand-tuned weights in YAML. M3's gate is lifted only by S3.4: without a recommendation of instruments (confirmed or a rule candidate) the score is at most 0.4, so personalization alone is weak and education is low. This replaces plan §6's "no S3.4 and no S3.3".
- **Findings** in the `Finding` v1 shape: `module` M1/M3, `firm` (M1 only), `advertiser`, `rules` with S1.x/S3.x ids, a Polish title and summary, and the raw `score`. `Finding.advertiser` (`AdvertiserRef`) is added to the models by `analytics-worker` (its follow-up #3, from the `analytics-skeleton` follow-up #1). This change fills it. If the worker lands without it, this change adds it.
- **LLM seam**: this change uses the seam that the sibling stage-2 change `analytics-llm-p0` defines (its design §2): the shared `Rule`/`Catalogue`/`run_rules` engine, a YAML `llm:` block on S1.4, S1.5, S3.3 and S3.4, and the `judge` passed through `analyze`. The other signals have no `llm:` block, so the LLM never overrules them. Without a judge, or when it gives no verdict, M1/M3 stay rules-only with `model.llm = null`. This change does not build the LLM client, prompt, quote verification or cache; `analytics-llm-p0` does. The rules-only part (task groups 1-5) does not wait for it; wiring the judge into M1/M3 (group 6) starts after it is merged.
- **Tests** on the six M1/M3 mock documents in `contracts/mocks/documents.json` (ids `3c41441c…`, `182e5a42…`, `8721cb75…`, `007bbcee…`, `cdbf5306…`, `72acbb3b…`), checked against the signals in `contracts/mocks/findings.json` where rules alone can reach them, plus hand-made cases for the routing rules.
- **Out of scope**: the LLM client, prompts and cache (`analytics-llm-p0`); the M2 P0 rules R4, R6, R10, R14, R17, R18 (`analytics-llm-p0`); the topic gate and embeddings (plan §2 step 2); the KNF public warnings list (S3.7) and the real KNF loader; the gold set, `eval/run.py` and calibration (plan §8); OCR of images; the aggregator and its filters (API and web; the contract already carries `module`, `platform`, `detected_at` and `score`); any change to the worker, storage or events code; `.docs/STATUS.md`, which the coordinator updates.
- **Owner**: Damian (`analytics/`).
- **Contracts**: reads `ScrapedDocument` v1 (`author`, `links`, `url`, `text`) and writes `Finding` v1 with `module` M1/M3, `advertiser` and S-ids. Both are allowed by `contracts/schemas/finding.schema.json` v1 as it is (`rule_id` pattern `S[13].[0-9]+`, `advertiser` object), so there is no contract change and no version bump. No REST route changes. Events are unchanged: the worker already publishes `finding.created` and `finding.high_risk` per module with the M1 0.6 / M3 0.7 thresholds (ADR 0305).

## Dependencies

- **`analytics-worker` (stage 1, unmerged):** must land first. It adds `AdvertiserRef` and the upsert on `(document_id, module)`, which stores M1 and M3 findings of one document side by side.
- **`analytics-llm-p0` (stage 2 sibling):** independent for the rules-only groups; task group 6 (LLM wiring for M1/M3) depends on it being merged. Both changes touch `rules/engine.py` (additive: per-module id check, `KNOWN_PARAMS`, here also `Rule.evidence_fields`) and `pipeline.py`. Both modify the `document-analysis` requirement "M2 finding assembly" (this change only its unmatched-firm scenario), and this change also modifies "Routing to modules", so whichever archives second merges the requirement text against the main spec.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities
- `m1-third-party-signals`: M1 signals S1.1-S1.7, their evidence, the M1 confidence score and the M1 inventory finding (firm, advertiser, content, URL).
- `m3-unauthorized-advice-signals`: M3 signals S3.1-S3.8, the S3.4 gate, the M3 score, M3 alongside M1, and the M3 finding (entity data, offer content, URL).

### Modified Capabilities
- `document-analysis`: "Routing to modules" changes to publisher-and-registry routing (firm accounts to M2, unregistered own pages to M3, M1 plus M3 on one document). "M2 finding assembly" drops the scenario of an M2 finding for an unmatched `firm_own` page, since such pages no longer reach M2. Added requirements: registry match on link hosts for third-party content, the signal catalogue and its evidence sources, the advertiser on M1/M3 findings, and the LLM seam with rules-only degradation.
